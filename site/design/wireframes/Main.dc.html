How to read this site

Every record is a government record. Pending status comes from Job Bank's own "LMIA requested" flag, which the site reads as: the employer could not find a Canadian worker for this job and applied to hire a temporary foreign worker.

Indicators are pattern flags with written rules and known false positives. They are not findings. Government findings appear only under "Non-compliance findings", from IRCC's published list.

Any employer named here can submit a response, which appears on their profile. See Methodology and Corrections and right of reply.

Sources: Job Bank (ESDC); TFWP positive and negative LMIA employer lists (Open Government Licence, Canada); IRCC non-compliant employers; BC Registry. Download the data Corrections
