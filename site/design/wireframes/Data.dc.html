Licence and attribution

ESDC's positive and negative LMIA employer lists are published under the Open Government Licence, Canada. Job Bank content is Government of Canada material reproduced with attribution. Derived files on this site are released under the Open Government Licence, Canada as well, so anyone can check the work or build on it.

Scraping is polite: one request at a time, a named user agent with a contact address, and the nightly run stops if Job Bank returns errors.

Right of reply

An employer named on the site can submit a statement. It is posted unedited on the profile with the date received, after a check that the sender controls the contact details on the Job Bank posting or the registry record.

Corrections

Anyone can report a wrong match, a stale record, or an indicator that misfires. Each request gets a reply, and every change made is listed in the profile's corrections log.

About

This site collects public records about Labour Market Impact Assessments so that residents, workers and journalists can see which employers are applying to hire temporary foreign workers, where, and when, and which applications were approved or refused. It is run by [OPERATOR], independent of any government, employer or union.

Records are government data reproduced as published. Indicators are pattern flags defined on the Methodology page and are not findings of misconduct. Enforcement belongs to Service Canada and IRCC, and the site links to their reporting channels on every profile.

Contact: [EMAIL]. Privacy: the site stores no visitor accounts, sets no tracking cookies, and keeps correction and response submissions only as long as needed to act on them.
